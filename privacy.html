Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 02, 2026

Welcome to Little Master! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our educational games. We care deeply about children's privacy and safety and comply with the Google Play Families Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in keeping things simple and safe:

  • No Personal Data: We do NOT collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses.
  • Game Progress: We save your game level directly on your device using "Local Storage." This data stays on your phone/tablet.

2. Ads (Unity Ads & Start.io)

Note: This app uses third-party advertising services to keep the app free.

We use Unity Ads (for video ads) and Start.io (for banner ads). Since this app is designed for children, we work with partners who support family-friendly ad filtering.

Data Collection by Ad Networks:

These services may collect anonymous data (like device type, advertising ID, or IP address) strictly for internal operations such as:

  • Fraud prevention (stopping fake clicks).
  • Frequency capping (ensuring you don't see the same ad too often).

For more details, please review their privacy policies:

  • Unity Ads: Read Policy
  • Start.io: Read Policy

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Play Policy)

Our app is strictly designed for kids and families. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • The app does not use the camera or microphone.
